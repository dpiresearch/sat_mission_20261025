Assumptions

  • Spacecraft RF (AMSAT LTM): 100 mW telemetry only, 450 mW telemetry plus transponder. The transponder's 350 mW is shared among 5 simultaneous SSB users.
  • Spacecraft antennas: −3 dBi effective for a tumbling monopole or dipole (an allowance for pattern nulls) and 1 dB line loss. The 2 m element is TBD (see open items).
  • Spacecraft receive noise: 700 K for an Earth-facing VHF antenna (about 290 K Earth plus man-made noise) and the LNA.
  • Propagation: 3 dB polarization loss (linear spacecraft vs circular ground, or Faraday fading) and 0.5 dB atmospheric and ionospheric loss.
  • Required SNR: 10 dB in 2.4 kHz for SSB, 6 dB in 500 Hz for CW. BPSK needs Eb/N0 of 9.6 dB (uncoded 10⁻⁵) plus 1.5 dB implementation loss. This is conservative; FoxTelem's FEC improves on it.
  • Ground gains are typical amateur values (handheld 2 m / 70 cm Yagi, crossed Yagis with LNA) and have not been checked against vendor data.
